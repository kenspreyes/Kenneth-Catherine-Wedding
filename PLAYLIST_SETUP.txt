OUR WEDDING PLAYLIST — HOW TO ADD YOUR SONGS

1. Create the folder assets/audio/ in your wedding website project.
2. Put your legally obtained MP3 audio files inside it.
3. Name the first three files:
   track-01.mp3
   track-02.mp3
   track-03.mp3
4. In script.js, find the V8.76 TRACKS array near the end.
   Replace Song 01 / Song 02 / Song 03 with the real song titles.
   You can also replace the placeholder artist names.
   Add more objects to TRACKS and matching HTML rows as needed.
5. Test with Live Server. Audio only plays after the visitor presses Play.
6. Commit and push the files, including assets/audio/, to GitHub Pages.

No copyrighted music is included. Only publish files you have permission to distribute.
Music keeps playing while guests scroll on the same page, subject to
browser and operating-system media policies. It will stop on a full reload
or when navigating to another website.
